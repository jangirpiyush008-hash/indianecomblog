## Section 3 — Top 10 Shortlist (ranked)

### 1. Dhammak (धम्मक)
**Meaning:** "With a bang / impact" — Hindi colloquialism signalling arrival, force, presence.
**Strength:** .com, .in, .co ALL available — only candidate with triple-TLD ownership possible. Distinct, memorable, culturally Indian, works globally once phonetically introduced.
**Risk:** Spelling will need coaching for non-Hindi speakers initially.

### 2. Indiopia
**Meaning:** India + Utopia. The ideal India, discovered daily.
**Strength:** .com + .in both available. Instantly signals Indian focus. Modern portmanteau.
**Risk:** Slightly longer; "utopia" reference may feel aspirational-grand.

### 3. Haalchal (हालचाल)
**Meaning:** "What's going on" / status / news — a greeting every Indian uses.
**Strength:** Everyday vocabulary. Instantly familiar. .in + .co available (not .com).
**Risk:** No .com — forces Indian-primary positioning (may be fine, may be limiting).

### 4. Qrux
**Meaning:** The crux of what's new, stylised.
**Strength:** Modern, tech-brand aesthetic, .in + .co available.
**Risk:** "Q-brand" fatigue (Quora, Qatar Airways, many Q startups).

### 5. Fybls
**Meaning:** Stylised "fables" — the brand tells India's unfolding fables.
**Strength:** .com + .in both available, rare for a 5-letter name.
**Risk:** Will be constantly misspelled / mispronounced ("fibbles"? "fy-buhls"?).

### 6. Vayura
**Meaning:** Vayu (wind) + aura. News with air-speed and presence.
**Strength:** .in available. Lyrical, premium-sounding.
**Risk:** .com taken limits global SEO.

### 7. Khoj
**Meaning:** "Discovery / search" in Hindi.
**Strength:** Semantically perfect for a discovery platform. .co available.
**Risk:** Only .co — no .com or .in. Limits brand ownership.

### 8. Nuvla
**Meaning:** Modern cloud-adjacent portmanteau.
**Strength:** .in available. Clean.
**Risk:** Weak Indian cultural signal; sounds European.

### 9. Hoshiyar
**Meaning:** "Clever / savvy" in Hindi/Urdu.
**Strength:** .in available. Flatters the reader ("you're the hoshiyar one").
**Risk:** Long (9 letters); older demographic word.

### 10. Chirya
**Meaning:** Sparrow — small bird that spreads news.
**Strength:** .in available. Charming, approachable.
**Risk:** Diminutive feel may undercut "premium" positioning.

## Section 4 — Top 3 Recommendations (Full Briefs)

### 🥇 Recommendation 1: **DHAMMAK**

**Brand story:** Dhammak is the sound of something arriving with presence. It is the headline that makes you look up. It is the product launch India talks about tomorrow. We don't do quiet. We do dhammak — the noise, the news, the discoveries, the deals that India can't ignore.

**Visual identity direction:**
- Typeface: Bold serif display (Canela, Boska, or custom) for the wordmark; clean Inter/Satoshi for UI.
- Palette: Warm ivory (#FAF7F2) background; deep ink charcoal (#1A1A1A); single accent of sindoor red (#C8372D) or electric saffron (#F97316). Strong red is historically "attention" in Indian media.
- Logo: Possible chop-mark or stamp motif (echoes छाप / impression). Could use a subtle Devanagari ligature in the "a" letters for Indian identity.
- Photography: High-contrast editorial, warm skin tones, Indian faces/places front-and-centre.

**Tagline options:**
- "India, with a bang."
- "Loud enough to matter."
- "The news India can't ignore."
- "Dhammak se." ("With impact.")

**Domain status (2026-11-25 verified):**
- dhammak.com — ✅ AVAILABLE
- dhammak.in — ✅ AVAILABLE
- dhammak.co — ✅ AVAILABLE

**Trademark/risk notes:**
- No obvious global trademark conflicts found in casual search; a formal clearance search (India IP Office + USPTO + EUIPO) is required before launch.
- "Dhammak" is a Hindi colloquialism, not a registered word — unlikely to face generic-term objections.
- Social handle check: verify @dhammak on Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn — do before domain purchase.

**Why pick this one:** Triple-TLD availability is extraordinarily rare for a short, pronounceable, culturally Indian word. This is the only candidate where we can own `.com`, `.in`, and `.co` simultaneously — a strategic brand asset.

## Section 5 — Trademark & Risk Notes

### Pre-launch checks required (any winner)

1. **Indian Trademark Registry search** via public portal (https://ipindiaonline.gov.in) — classes 9 (digital publications), 35 (advertising/e-commerce), 38 (telecommunications), 41 (education/entertainment).
2. **USPTO search** (https://tmsearch.uspto.gov) — same classes.
3. **EUIPO search** (https://euipo.europa.eu) — same classes.
4. **Google / Bing / DuckDuckGo** for competing media brands using the name.
5. **Social handle availability:** Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest.
6. **ASCI compliance check:** Advertising Standards Council of India has disclosure rules for sponsored/affiliate content — brand name should not imply guarantees ("Best", "Genuine", etc. in the mark).

### Specific risk flags

- **Dhammak:** None material found; proceed to formal clearance.
- **Indiopia:** None found; confirm no startup has quietly filed intent-to-use.
- **Haalchal:** Risk of dictionary-word rejection in word-mark filing — rely on logo/stylised-mark filing. Check for competing Indian apps (quick Play Store search advised).

### Domain purchase strategy

Once a winner is chosen:
1. Register all three TLDs on the same day via a single registrar (Namecheap, Porkbun, or Dynadot).
2. Enable WHOIS privacy from day one.
3. Pre-register common misspellings and social handles the same day.
4. Set 2-year minimum registration with auto-renew.

### Do NOT

- Purchase any domain before I give written approval.
- File a trademark before legal counsel reviews.
- Announce the brand publicly before social handles and domains are locked.

## Methodology Appendix

**Domain availability was verified using:**
1. `dig +short NS <domain>` — empty NS record strongly indicates unregistered status.
2. `whois <domain>` cross-check — confirms via registrar records.
3. A domain was marked `AVAILABLE` only when both methods returned empty/no-registration.
4. Cases where NS was empty but whois showed registrar data were classified `TAKEN (whois)`.

**Total domains queried:** ~290 across 168 candidate names and three TLDs.
**Verified available:** 25 candidate-TLD combinations.
**Top-tier (multi-TLD + strong brand):** 3 — Dhammak, Indiopia, Haalchal.
